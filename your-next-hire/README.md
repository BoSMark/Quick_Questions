# Your Next Hire

Money is no object. Who is your next hire?

A conversation, usually 5 to 10 minutes, that starts with that question and follows your answer to what it actually reveals about your company's stage and what you believe is holding it back.

You get a Next Hire Brief at the end: the role, what changes once it's filled, and what your answer implies about where the company actually is.

---

## Install

1. Download [`your-next-hire.skill`](./your-next-hire.skill)
2. Open Claude → Settings → Capabilities → Install skill
3. Start a new conversation and say "next hire"

---

## What comes next

If you have the BoS OS plugin installed in Cowork, just say "run the Bootstrap skill" and go straight into it. Otherwise, run the [BoS OS Bootstrap](https://github.com/BoSMark/BoS_OS_Start) skill yourself. It builds a picture of your company from public information that will help the BoS OS ask you some meaningful questions.
